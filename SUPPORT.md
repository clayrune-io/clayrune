# Getting help

Clayrune is maintained by one person. There is no support desk, but there is a
real human reading everything below, and a reply is the normal outcome.

## Something is broken

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ronle/clayrune/issues/new/choose). If the
installer stopped part-way, paste the last line it printed. That one line
usually identifies the problem on its own.

## It is not a bug

[The same place](https://github.com/ronle/clayrune/issues/new/choose), under
"Feedback, a question, or an idea". Questions about whether Clayrune does a
particular thing are welcome and are usually a sign the docs are missing
something.

## You would rather not post in public

Email <leviran1@gmail.com>. Nothing about a private message makes it less
useful.

## Before you install

There is a simulated walkthrough at <https://clayrune.io/demo> that runs
entirely in the browser. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves the page.

## What we cannot help with

Clayrune runs your own agents on your own machine against your own Claude
subscription. Problems with Claude itself, with your subscription, or with the
Claude CLI are outside what this project can fix, though it is still worth
asking if you are not sure which one you are looking at.
